Tips for a good complaint

Be specific. Mention exact room, time and a short subject to help the team triage faster.

Add a photo if possible. Visuals help speed up diagnosis.

Choose the right category — it helps routing to the correct team.

What happens after submission?

Complaints are reviewed by the administration and assigned to a team. You will receive an update when the status changes. Typical response time is within 48 hours.
